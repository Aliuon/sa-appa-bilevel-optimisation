Figure 2b. Data profile (More and Wild, 2009) across all 44 reconciled instances x 30 runs = 1320 (instance,run) pairs, at relative-error tolerance tau=0.25. f_L(i) is the same empirical best-found value used by Figure 2 (no certified global optimum is available uniformly across the suite); f_0(i) is the mean best-feasible objective at the end of the initial N0=25-point LHS design, averaged over all methods/runs on instance i. A run is solved at budget kappa (measured in simplex gradients, kappa_evals/(n_ul(i)+1)) once its running-best feasible objective first satisfies f <= f_L(i) + tau*(f_0(i)-f_L(i)). Distinct from, and not to be conflated with, the Dolan-More performance profile of Figure 2, which fixes the final budget and varies tau instead.
