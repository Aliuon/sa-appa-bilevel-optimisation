Critical-difference diagram, Scenario 8 (Published surrogate-assisted bilevel method): SA-APPA vs a matched-budget re-implementation of BL-SAEA (Jiang et al., IEEE TCYB 2024). Methods M1, M14, n=44 instances. Omnibus Friedman test (Conover's F-approximation): T2=37.355, F(1,43), p=2.514e-07. Nemenyi critical difference CD=0.295 at alpha=0.05 (Demsar, 2006 -- a distinct post-hoc procedure to the Conover paired critical difference reported alongside the omnibus test in Appendix D, not interchangeable with it; the Nemenyi CD here is not currently traced to a bibliography entry -- see the standing citation-verification note). Methods are positioned by mean rank (lower/left = better); a solid bar connects methods whose mean-rank difference is below the Nemenyi CD, i.e.\ not distinguishable at this significance level.
