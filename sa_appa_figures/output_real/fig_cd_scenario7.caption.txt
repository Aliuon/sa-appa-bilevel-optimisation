Methods M1, M12, M13, n=44 instances. Omnibus Friedman test (Conover's F-approximation): T2=0.436, F(2,86), p=0.6479. Nemenyi critical difference CD=0.500 at alpha=0.05 (Demsar, 2006 -- a distinct post-hoc procedure to the Conover paired critical difference reported alongside the omnibus test in Appendix D, not interchangeable with it; the Nemenyi CD here is not currently traced to a bibliography entry -- see the standing citation-verification note). Methods are positioned by mean rank (lower/left = better); a solid bar connects methods whose mean-rank difference is below the Nemenyi CD, i.e.\ not distinguishable at this significance level.
