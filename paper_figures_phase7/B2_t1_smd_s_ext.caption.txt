Evaluations to target, SMD1-SMD12 at (p, q) = (3, 3), Phase II comparators (M1, M10-M14). Bar: median over the 30 runs (not 31) of the number of full nested LL solves after which the best feasible UL objective first lies within 0.01 (absolute) of the reference value F_ref; F_ref is the best value found by any configuration in any run, except on SMD1-SMD9 where F_ref = 0, the known UL optimum. Runs that never reach the target are censored; where fewer than 15 of the 30 runs reach it the median is undefined and 'target not reached' is written instead of a bar. The budget is 60 nested solves. Success counts are in the companion table. Counts are in nested solves, the study's cost unit; LL function evaluations per solve are in the budget-composition figure.
