TP3, SA-APPA (M1), run 5 (seed 5), selected by a rule fixed in advance: the lower median (15th of 30) of the locked final UL objectives. The GP is the one SA-APPA held at the end of the run (60 nested solves), trained on 60 truly evaluated points with its own settings (squared-exponential kernel, lengthscale 7.81 from the median heuristic, signal variance 1, outputs mean-centred and not rescaled); no hyperparameter was tuned for this figure. Absolute error of the GP mean on a 61 x 61 grid, with training points (dots), the optimum (open circle), the UL constraint boundaries (black) and the LL kink lines x_i = 10 (dotted). The error is largest at (14.2, 15.0), away from the training points; inside the feasible region it is smaller but does not vanish near the kink x_2 = 10, on which the optimum lies, because the smooth kernel cannot represent the change of slope there.
