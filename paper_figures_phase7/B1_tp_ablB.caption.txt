Budget composition, TP1-TP9, internal ablation, constraint handling and step-size rule (M1, M7-M9). Each bar is the median over runs 0-4 (5 of the 30 locked runs, re-run under the same seeds with a counter on the LL objective f) of the UL evaluations (solid lower segment; 60 full nested solves for every configuration) plus the LL objective evaluations made inside those solves (hatched upper segment). Every LL solve is a nested heuristic solve: SciPy differential evolution with population N_ll = 6 n_ll, at most T_ll = 20 generations and L-BFGS-B polishing (M14 changes only its initial population); none is exact. The y axis is logarithmic, so segment heights are not proportional to counts; the UL share is under 1% of the total in every case. 4 re-run(s) that did not reproduce the locked trajectory within 1e-6 (relative) were left out of the median.
