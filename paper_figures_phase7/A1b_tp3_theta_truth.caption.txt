The LL problem min ||x - y||^2 over y in [0, 10]^2 is a strictly convex quadratic programme, so y*(x) = min(x, 10) is single-valued, as Assumption A2 requires; it was solved by bounded-variable least squares (SciPy lsq_linear, 'bvls') and checked against the closed-form projection. The kinks at x_i = 10 are where the LL bound becomes active. The open circle marks the bilevel optimum x* = (15, 10), y* = (10, 10), theta = 325, f* = 25; the blue lines on the floor are the UL constraint boundaries x1 + 2 x2 = 30 (solid) and x1 + x2 = 25 (dashed).
