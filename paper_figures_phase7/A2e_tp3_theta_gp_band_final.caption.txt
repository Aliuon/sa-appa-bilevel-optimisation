TP3, SA-APPA (M1), run 5 (seed 5), selected by a rule fixed in advance: the lower median (15th of 30) of the locked final UL objectives. The GP is the one SA-APPA held at the end of the run (60 nested solves), trained on 60 truly evaluated points with its own settings (squared-exponential kernel, lengthscale 7.81 from the median heuristic, signal variance 1, outputs mean-centred and not rescaled); no hyperparameter was tuned for this figure. Uncertainty of the GP: the surfaces mu +/- 2 sigma (multiplier 2) and the true theta. Because the signal variance is fixed at 1 while theta varies by about 1032, sigma is at most 1 and averages 0.0027; the two surfaces are indistinguishable at this scale. The empirical coverage of the +/- 2 sigma interval on 2,500 held-out points is 0.2% over the box and 0.0% within 1.5 of x*, against a nominal 95%: the GP's predictive uncertainty is not calibrated on this problem.
