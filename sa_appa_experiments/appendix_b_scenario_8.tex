\paragraph{Friedman test summary (Scenario 8).}

Critical values: $F_{0.05}(1,43)=4.067$ (reject $H_0$ at $\alpha=0.05$); $F_{0.01}(1,43)=7.264$ (reject $H_0$ at $\alpha=0.01$). Mean ranks: SA-APPA=1.841, M14 (BL-SAEA re-impl.)=1.159. Conover post-hoc paired critical difference (rank-sum units): $CD_{0.05}=9.899$, $CD_{0.01}=13.229$. Nemenyi critical distance (mean-rank units, for the CD diagram, Figure~\ref{fig:cd8}): $CD_{0.05}=0.295$.
