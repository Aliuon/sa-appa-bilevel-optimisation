\paragraph{Friedman test summary (Scenario 3).}
$n=44$ instances, $k=4$ methods. $A_2=1312.500$, $B_2=51152.500$, $T_2=17.9397$, distributed $F(3,129)$ under $H_0$, $p=8.601e-10$. Critical values: $F_{0.05}(3,129)=2.675$ (reject $H_0$ at $\alpha=0.05$); $F_{0.01}(3,129)=3.937$ (reject $H_0$ at $\alpha=0.01$). Mean ranks: M3 (bare PPA)=2.364, M4 (GA engine)=2.580, M5 (PSO engine)=3.364, M6 (DE engine)=1.693. Conover post-hoc paired critical difference (rank-sum units): $CD_{0.05}=20.010$, $CD_{0.01}=26.442$. Nemenyi critical distance (mean-rank units, for the CD diagram, Figure~\ref{fig:cd3}): $CD_{0.05}=0.707$.
