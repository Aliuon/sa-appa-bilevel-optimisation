\paragraph{Friedman test summary (Scenario 3).}

Mean ranks: M3 (bare PPA)=2.341, M4 (GA engine)=2.557, M5 (DE engine)=3.409, M6 (PSO engine)=1.693. Conover post-hoc paired critical difference (rank-sum units): $CD_{0.05}=19.761$, $CD_{0.01}=26.113$. Nemenyi critical distance (mean-rank units, for the CD diagram, Figure~\ref{fig:cd3}): $CD_{0.05}=0.707$.
