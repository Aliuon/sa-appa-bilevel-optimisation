Conover post-hoc paired critical difference (rank-sum units): $CD_{0.05}=13.289$, $CD_{0.01}=17.759$. Nemenyi critical distance (mean-rank units, for the CD diagram, Figure~\ref{fig:cd1}): $CD_{0.05}=0.295$.
