Figure 8 (Scenario 3, CD3). Critical-difference diagram, Scenario 3 (Search engine): PPA engine vs GA/PSO/DE (M3, bare PPA, used as the PPA representative so Scenario 2's genetic-operator effect is not confounded with the engine comparison). Methods M3, M4, M5, M6, n=44 instances. Omnibus Friedman test (Conover's F-approximation): T2=17.940, F(3,129), p=8.601e-10. Nemenyi critical difference CD=0.707 at alpha=0.05 (Demsar, 2006 -- a distinct post-hoc procedure to the Conover paired critical difference reported alongside the omnibus test in Appendix D, not interchangeable with it; the Nemenyi CD here is not currently traced to a bibliography entry -- see the standing citation-verification note). Methods are positioned by mean rank (lower/left = better); a solid bar connects methods whose mean-rank difference is below the Nemenyi CD, i.e.\ not distinguishable at this significance level. Mean ranks: M6=1.693, M3=2.364, M4=2.580, M5=3.364.
